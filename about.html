About My Private Chef

My Private Chef was created around a simple idea: people should be able to enjoy freshly prepared meals even when they do not have enough time to cook.

Daily life can become busy. Students may spend long days at school or university, working professionals may work long hours, and families may have many responsibilities. My Private Chef gives customers another option by allowing them to plan their meals before they need them.

Customers can choose from breakfast, lunch and dinner options, select their preferred delivery dates and have their meals delivered to convenient locations.

Our aim is not to replace home cooking. Instead, we provide a convenient meal option for days when customers do not have the time or opportunity to prepare their own food.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday meals easier by providing fresh, delicious and convenient food that can be planned around each customer's schedule.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted meal preparation and delivery service that helps customers enjoy freshly prepared meals without the stress of cooking every day.
