## Project Overview

My Private Chef is a fictional meal preparation and delivery business based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

The business provides freshly prepared breakfast, lunch and dinner meals for customers who want convenient food without having to cook every day or depend on fast food.

Customers must place orders at least one day in advance. They can also plan meals for several days, weeks or an entire month and choose their preferred delivery dates and available delivery times.

Meals can be delivered to homes, workplaces, schools and universities within selected service areas.

The website was created as part of the WEDE5020 Portfolio of Evidence.

## Website Goals and Objectives

The main goals of the website are to:

- Introduce My Private Chef to potential customers.
- Explain the meal preparation and delivery services offered.
- Display breakfast, lunch and dinner meal options.
- Allow customers to make enquiries.
- Provide information about delivery areas and operating hours.
- Make it easy for users to navigate between website pages.
