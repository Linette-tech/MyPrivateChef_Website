Orders must be placed at least one day before the selected delivery date.

Meals can be delivered to:

  • Homes
  • Workplaces
  • Schools
  • Universities
  • Other approved locations within our service areas

Customers planning several meals in advance can select different delivery dates for their meals.

Please provide the correct delivery information when making an enquiry to help prevent delivery delays.
